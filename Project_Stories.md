# SUBs_TRACKt

## Status

Active

---

## One Sentence

A commitment intelligence platform that helps people understand recurring financial commitments before they silently consume future income.

---

## The Problem

Many people know what subscriptions they pay for.

Far fewer understand the broader concept of recurring financial commitments.

Subscriptions, memberships, installment payments, insurance, recurring donations, school fees, domain renewals, and similar obligations all reduce future disposable income.

Most people only notice them after money has already left their account.

---

## The Insight

The real problem is not subscription management.

It is commitment awareness.

People need visibility into future obligations before money disappears.

---

## Goal

Create a system that discovers recurring commitments and helps people understand where future income is already committed.

---

## Approach

Rather than asking users to manually enter subscriptions, the platform identifies commitments from trusted evidence such as emails and asks users to confirm them before building a dashboard.

Transparency and trust are central principles.

# Project Writing Rules

Every project should answer these questions.

1. What problem exists?

2. Why does it matter?

3. Why did I choose to solve it?

4. What approach did I take?

5. What obstacles did I encounter?

6. What did I learn?

7. How has this project influenced the way I build software today?

Technology should always appear near the end.

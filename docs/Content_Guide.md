# Content Guide

## Purpose

This document defines the messaging, storytelling, and content philosophy for the portfolio.

It exists to ensure every section communicates a consistent story regardless of which AI assistant is used during development.

The portfolio should never read like a resume.

Instead, it should feel like the personal website of someone who enjoys understanding difficult problems, learning deeply, and building thoughtful products.

---

# Overall Narrative

The portfolio should tell one continuous story.

That story is:

A curious learner became fascinated by technology, discovered Artificial Intelligence and Natural Language Processing, developed an interest in entrepreneurship, and now enjoys building software that solves meaningful problems.

Every section should reinforce this narrative.

---

# Tone of Voice

The writing should be:

- thoughtful
- calm
- conversational
- confident
- humble
- authentic
- intellectually curious

Avoid sounding corporate.

Avoid trying to impress with buzzwords.

Avoid exaggeration.

---

# Things to Avoid

Never write phrases like:

- Passionate software engineer
- Results-driven developer
- Highly motivated individual
- Fast learner
- Team player
- Detail-oriented
- Self-starter
- Hardworking developer

These are generic and do not communicate personality.

Instead, demonstrate these qualities through stories and projects.

---

# Hero

## Purpose

Immediately communicate identity.

Visitors should understand what kind of person I am before learning what technologies I know.

The hero should answer:

"Who is this person?"

rather than

"What framework does he use?"

The headline should communicate curiosity, product thinking, and craftsmanship.

The supporting paragraph should explain what motivates me to build.

---

# Journey

## Purpose

Explain how I arrived here.

The Journey section should feel personal.

It should communicate growth rather than achievements.

Suggested milestones include:

- Discovering programming through games
- Growing interested in mathematics
- Studying Computer Science
- Discovering AI
- Falling in love with NLP
- Becoming interested in entrepreneurship
- Building meaningful software

The focus should be evolution.

---

# Featured Projects

## Purpose

Projects are evidence of how I think.

Each project should tell a story.

Every project should answer:

What problem did I notice?

Why was it important?

What approach did I take?

What did I learn?

What happened afterward?

Technology should support the story.

Technology should never become the story.

---

# Experience

## Purpose

Communicate growth and impact.

Experience should focus on:

- problems solved
- responsibilities
- outcomes
- lessons learned

Avoid listing technologies without context.

---

# Writing Style

Sentences should generally be concise.

Prefer:

clarity

precision

simplicity

Avoid unnecessary complexity.

Long paragraphs should be broken into smaller ones.

---

# Storytelling Rules

Whenever describing a project, prefer this order:

Problem

->

Observation

->

Approach

->

Implementation

->

Outcome

->

Lessons Learned

This structure should remain consistent across the portfolio.

---

# Consistency Rules

Every section should reinforce at least one of these ideas:

- Curiosity
- Learning
- Product Thinking
- Problem Solving
- Entrepreneurship
- Thoughtful Engineering
- AI
- NLP

If content does not reinforce one of these themes, reconsider whether it belongs on the portfolio.

---

# Future Content

The portfolio should be easy to expand.

Future additions may include:

- New projects
- Research
- Articles
- Talks
- Reading notes
- Startup case studies
- Product essays
- Open source work

The information architecture should support growth without requiring a redesign.

---

# Final Guiding Principle

People should not leave remembering a list of technologies.

They should leave remembering how I think.

The portfolio should communicate that I am someone who enjoys understanding difficult problems, learning continuously, and building thoughtful products that improve people's lives.
